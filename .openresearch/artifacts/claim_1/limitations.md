# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

The general certificate is machine checked, but its trusted foundation
contains the standard Lebesgue-integral monotonicity and Fubini theorems. It
does not reduce those theorems to set theory or a third-party proof assistant.
The trust boundary is enumerated inline and in JSON. The uncountable graphing
is a genuine singular sparse operator, while numerical Fourier auditing is
only a regression for the symbolic Haar-invariance proof.

No tolerance, random seed, fitting, or formula-selected sample size is used.
