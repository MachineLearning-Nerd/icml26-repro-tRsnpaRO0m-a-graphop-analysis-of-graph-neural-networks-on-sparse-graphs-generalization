# Claim 4 limitations and deviations

- This is a proof-level falsification of the strict-subset clause at `L=0`,
  not an experimental challenge to compactness.
- It does not claim that strictness fails for `L>=1`.
- The finite grid enumeration is only a sanity check. The verdict rests on the
  arbitrary-measure pushforward argument, so it is not extrapolated from a toy
  domain.
- No stochastic sampling, tolerance, or formula-selected resource budget is
  used.
