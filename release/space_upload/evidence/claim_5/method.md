# Claim 5 method

Route 1: the realizable DIDM image `K` is compact inside the metric ambient
DIDM space, hence closed. Tietze extends any `F in C(K,R)` to the ambient
space. Ambient MPNN density approximates the extension uniformly, and
restriction to `K` cannot increase error.

Route 2: restrict the ambient MPNN algebra to `K`. It remains an algebra,
contains constants, and still separates points. Since `K` is compact
Hausdorff, Stone-Weierstrass gives uniform density directly.

Neither route uses the false proper-subset clause from Corollary 5.3.
