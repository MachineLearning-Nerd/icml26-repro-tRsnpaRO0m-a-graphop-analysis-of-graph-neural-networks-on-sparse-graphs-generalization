# Claim 4 method

Take the paper's universal quantifier literally and instantiate `L=0`. For an
arbitrary Borel probability measure `pi` on `H^0=[-1,1]^d`, set

```text
Omega=H^0,  mu=pi,  A=0,  f=id_H0.
```

The zero operator is linear, self-adjoint, positivity preserving, and has
induced norm zero, hence belongs to the `r`-bounded bofops for every `r>0`.
The identity is an admissible measurable signal. Definition 5.1 then gives
`gamma_0=id_H0`, so `Gamma_0=(id_H0)_* pi=pi`. Since `pi` was arbitrary, every
ambient order-zero DIDM is realizable. The reverse inclusion is definitional,
therefore `Gamma_0(BF_d^r)=P(H^0)`.

The verifier checks this quantified proof certificate. A separate checker
reconstructs the obligations and exhausts all 15 denominator-four probability
measures on a three-point feature grid as a nonessential sanity check.
