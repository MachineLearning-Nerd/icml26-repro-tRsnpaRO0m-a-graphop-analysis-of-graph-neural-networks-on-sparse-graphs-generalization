# Claim 3 limitations and deviations

- The falsification applies to the formally defined `MP_D` class and the
  theorem's stated dependency of `C'` only on `L,D,r`.
- If the authors intended an additional global range or value-at-zero bound,
  the counterexample would be outside that repaired class. No such condition
  appears in the formal class definition.
- Only the output inequality is needed to falsify the conjunctive theorem; no
  claim is made here about a repaired hidden-state inequality.
- The input action distance is conservatively upper-bounded, not estimated.
  This strengthens the contradiction and avoids numerical tolerance.
